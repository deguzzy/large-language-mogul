How it plays

A tycoon game about the most expensive science project on Earth

Real-time management you can pause whenever a training run spikes, a client calls or the power trips.

Pick your first model

Bet the company on a chat assistant, a vision model or a recommendation engine. Each path has its own clients, data needs and research tree.

Babysit training runs

Watch the loss curve, catch spikes, call early stopping, and survive the launch review before you ship.

Hire a founding team

Founders, ML, hardware and software engineers level up, fill skill slots and burn out if you push too hard.

Balance six resources

Cash, compute, talent, data, reputation and electricity all pull against each other. More GPUs means a bigger power bill.

Raise, borrow, survive

Land client contracts, license your models, take loans and pitch investors before the runway hits zero.

Outgrow the garage

Upgrade computers, furniture, building and decor on their own tracks, then race a rival lab to the next breakthrough.
